    Commonly Asked Queries

    What is the difference between NEXT, NATIONAL, CAPTAIN and SAPPHIRE?

    NEXT is our premium range made from virgin plastic. NATIONAL uses semi-virgin and reprocessed plastic across a wide range of price points. CAPTAIN and SAPPHIRE are our economical reprocessed-plastic ranges, built for affordable everyday use.

    Where can I buy National Plasto furniture?

    Our furniture is sold through a network of distributors and dealers across India. Use the Dealer Desk page to find a dealer near you, or send us an enquiry and our team will connect you.

    Do you supply bulk and institutional orders?

    Yes. We supply schools, offices, hotels, restaurants, event organisers and other institutions. Share your requirement through the enquiry form and our sales team will get back to you.

    Is plastic furniture suitable for outdoor use?

    Moulded plastic furniture does not rust or absorb water, which makes it well suited to verandas, gardens and outdoor dining. Storing it in the shade when not in use helps it keep its colour for longer.

    How do I clean and maintain plastic furniture?

    Wipe it with a soft cloth and mild soap and water. Avoid abrasive scrubbers and harsh chemicals so the surface finish stays smooth.

    Are National Plasto plants certified?

    Yes. All four of our plants are independently certified, with 23 current certificates covering ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 50001, CE and BIFMA.

    Which colours are available?

    Most models come in several colours. Open any product in the list above to see all of its colour options.
