Production Capabilities

MANUFACTURING EXCELLENCE

Advanced manufacturing. Consistent quality. Reliable scale. Operating integrated injection moulding plants engineered for high throughput and tight dimensional repeatability.

EXPLORE MANUFACTURING →

High-Tonnage Presses

Microprocessor injection moulding machines ranging up to 1,200T clamp capacity for single-shot monoblock articles.

Tool & Die Engineering

Hardened multi-cavity CNC-machined steel moulds with conformal cooling circuits ensuring rapid cycles and zero warping.

Automated Feed & Dosing

Dehumidifying hopper dryers and gravimetric masterbatch dosers guaranteeing batch-to-batch color and mechanical uniformity.

Integrated Plant Logistics

Dedicated warehousing bays and palletized dispatch terminals connected directly to India's major freight corridors.

Rigorous Testing

QUALITY YOU CAN TRUST

Auditable quality protocols validated across six distinct manufacturing inspection checkpoints to ensure zero structural or finish compromise.

Checkpoint 01

Raw Material Testing

Melt Flow Index (MFI) and density spectrometry verify only prime-grade polymer batches enter our injection barrels.

MFI Tolerance < 2%
Checkpoint 02

Process Automation

Continuous sensor logging of melt temperatures, injection pressure ramps, and mold chiller cooling uniformity.

Real-Time Sensor Logging
Checkpoint 03

Dimensional Accuracy

Calibrated dial calipers verify wall thickness, rib angles, and stacking clearances against master 3D CAD files.

Micron Tolerance Audits
Checkpoint 04

Optical Finish Inspection

100% visual inspection under high-CRI inspection lamps for color consistency, flow lines, and gloss uniformity.

High-CRI Optical Scan
Checkpoint 05

Destructive & Load Testing

Drop-impact testing from 1.5 meters and 200 kg static load hold cycles replicate real institutional stress conditions.

200 kg Static Hold Certified
Checkpoint 06

Pre-Dispatch Audit

Final pre-dispatch inspection verifying heavy protective wrap integrity, barcode SKU labelling, and nested stacks.

100% Traceable Lot QA
