## ✅ UX/UI Strengths

1. **Clear Value Proposition**: Homepage immediately communicates scale (₹11K Cr revenue, 35 plants, 55+ countries)
2. **Well-Organized Navigation**: Logical mega-menu structure covering all key areas
3. **Data-Driven Credibility**: Key metrics prominently displayed with animated counters
4. **Investor-Focused**: Dedicated investor desk with all regulatory documents
5. **Professional Photography**: High-quality images across all product sections
6. **Consistent Branding**: Uniform color scheme and typography throughout
7. **Multi-Stakeholder Coverage**: Addresses customers, investors, job seekers, media

---

## ⚠️ Areas for Improvement

1. **Mobile Experience**: Heavy use of animations and large images may slow mobile load
2. **Search Functionality**: No visible global search bar for product discovery
3. **E-Commerce / Dealer Locator**: Could benefit from an interactive dealer/distributor locator map
4. **Product Configurator**: No interactive product selection/specification tools
5. **Accessibility**: Corporate sites often lack WCAG compliance (contrast, alt text, screen reader support)
6. **Chat Support**: No live chat or chatbot for immediate customer queries
7. **Content Depth**: Product pages could benefit from more detailed technical specifications
8. **Page Load Speed**: Rich media-heavy pages could benefit from better lazy loading and optimization

---

## 🔧 Technical Observations

- **Framework**: Likely built on a PHP/CMS (WordPress or custom CMS)
- **Animations**: CSS transitions and scroll-triggered counter animations
- **Responsive**: Desktop-first design with responsive breakpoints
- **SSL**: HTTPS enabled (secure)
- **Localization**: English-only; no multilingual support despite 55+ country exports
- **PDF Downloads**: Investor relations documents downloadable directly
