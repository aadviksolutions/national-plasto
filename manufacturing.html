We engineer precision moulded furniture, heavy-duty material handling crates, and high-volume industrial components with zero compromise on structural integrity.

    Innovation: A key to success

    Product innovation and continuous upgradation of quality have been our consistent endeavours. Our team of dedicated experts in R&D innovates new products by keeping a keen eye on the current needs and trends of the market. These path-breaking new and innovative products have successfully replaced conventional products at an affordable cost. This has helped National Plasto to hold a leading voice in the industry.

    What Sets Us Apart?

    Our approach combines engineering validation, manufacturing precision and proven field experience. Our designs are validated through FEA, advanced multi-cavity tooling and actual field tests, with field data informing continuous improvements to furniture product design. Stringent process controls and continuous monitoring help us maintain less than 0.0001% rejections. Backed by a qualified engineering team and supplies to major commercial, agricultural and industrial sectors across India, we have built a consistent track record in quality. With National Plasto Products in the market since 1997 and pan-India reach across West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, and Odisha, we bring proven experience to every engagement while staying committed to building lasting customer relationships.
