    Rooted in 1942, Incorporated in 1997

    Over eight decades of industrial heritage and nearly three decades of pioneering polymer injection moulding excellence.

    Tracing foundational commercial roots back to 1942, National Plasto Products Pvt. Ltd. (NPPL) was incorporated on 29 October 1997 under the visionary leadership of Mr. Ratan Kumar Sharma. From an initial manufacturing plant in Kolkata, West Bengal, NPPL has expanded into a nationwide polymer manufacturing leader with 4 advanced manufacturing hubs across India. Operating high-tonnage microprocessor injection moulding machinery from 80T up to 1,200T across over 100,000 sq. ft. of production floor space, we design and produce over 1,000 active moulds and products. Our 4-tier brand architecture—NEXT, NATIONAL, SAPPHIRE, and CAPTAIN—delivers ergonomic, durable, and aesthetically superior moulded furniture to millions of Indian homes, institutions, and businesses.

    Vision

    To be India's most trusted polymer brand, setting the benchmark for ergonomic comfort, structural durability, and sustainable plastic manufacturing accessible to every home and enterprise.

    Mission

    Delivering high-value, long-lasting polymer solutions through continuous technological innovation, rigorous 200kg load testing, and an empowered pan-India network of over 500 distribution partners.

    What makes us National Plasto?

    National Plasto holds its pre-eminent position across India through unrelenting manufacturing precision, continuous polymer research, and long-standing dealer partnerships that touch millions of everyday lives.

    4 Comprehensive Brand Portfolios

    Through four strategically defined brands—NEXT (100% prime virgin polymer designer furniture), NATIONAL (flagship mass-market furniture), SAPPHIRE (high-gloss value range), and CAPTAIN (heavy-duty event & commercial seating)—National Plasto caters to every consumer demographic, budget tier, and architectural requirement across India with over 1,000 product variants.

    High-Tonnage Injection Moulding

    Our facilities house microprocessor-controlled injection moulding presses ranging from 80T to 1,200T. Backed by an in-house tooling room with EDM spark erosion and CNC cavity machining, we eliminate flash lines, ensure uniform wall thicknesses, and achieve flawless structural ribbing for superior load-bearing capacity.

    4 Strategic Manufacturing Hubs

    With integrated plants situated in Howrah (West Bengal - HQ), Guwahati (Assam), Roorkee (Uttarakhand), and Hajipur (Bihar), our decentralized production footprint spans East, North-East, and North India. This strategic positioning slashes transit turnaround times to 24–48 hours for over 500 distributors across 24 states.

    Rigorous 200kg Load Testing & Quality Standards

    Every National Plasto chair is engineered with high-impact polymer formulation, UV weather stabilizers, and reinforced stress-point ribs tested to withstand up to 200 kg of static and dynamic loads. Certified with ISO 9001:2015 across all plants, our quality assurance guarantees zero defects from resin feed to final dispatch.
