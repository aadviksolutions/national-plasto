    Rooted in 1981, Incorporated in 1997

    Over four decades of industrial heritage and nearly three decades of pioneering plastic injection moulding excellence.

    Tracing foundational commercial roots back to 1981, National Plasto Products Pvt. Ltd. (NPPL) was incorporated on 29 October 1997 under the visionary leadership of Mr. Ratan Kumar Sharma. From an initial manufacturing plant in Kolkata, West Bengal, NPPL has expanded into a nationwide plastic furniture manufacturing leader with 4 advanced manufacturing hubs across India. Operating high-tonnage microprocessor injection moulding machinery from 80T up to 1,200T across over 2,00,000 sq. ft. of production floor space, we design and produce over 1,000 active moulds and products. Our 4-tier brand architecture—NEXT, NATIONAL, SAPPHIRE, and CAPTAIN—delivers ergonomic, durable, and aesthetically superior moulded furniture to millions of Indian homes, institutions, and businesses.

    Vision

    To be India's most trusted plastic-moulded furniture brand, setting the benchmark for ergonomic comfort, structural durability, and sustainable plastic manufacturing accessible to every home and enterprise.

    Mission

    Delivering high-value, long-lasting plastic-moulded furniture solutions through continuous technological innovation, rigorous 200kg load testing, and an empowered pan-India network of over 500 distribution partners.

    What makes us National Plasto?

    National Plasto holds its pre-eminent position across India through unrelenting manufacturing precision, advanced plastic moulding technology, and long-standing dealer partnerships that touch millions of everyday lives.

    Our Management

    Since 1997, National Plasto Products Pvt. Ltd. (NPPL) has been manufacturing plastic moulded furniture designed for every space and every need. From homes and educational institutions to healthcare facilities, government establishments and businesses across the country, our products have become part of everyday environments across India.

    What began as a single manufacturing unit has grown into four manufacturing plants located in Howrah (West Bengal), Guwahati (Assam), Roorkee (Uttarakhand) and Hajipur (Bihar). All NPPL manufacturing units are ISO certified across various levels, reflecting our commitment to consistent processes and quality standards.

    Behind this growth is a family-led management team that brings together decades of hands-on experience, strong operational knowledge and clear defined responsibilities across finance, production, sales and marketing.
