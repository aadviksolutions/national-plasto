    Privacy Policy

    National Plasto Products Pvt. Ltd. (“NPPL”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

    Information we collect

    We only collect the details you choose to share with us — for example through our enquiry, dealership or catalogue forms, by email, or on WhatsApp. This may include your name, company name, phone number, email address, city, state, pincode and the contents of your message.

    How we use it

    • To respond to your enquiry, quotation request or dealership application.
    • To connect you with an authorised dealer or distributor near you.
    • To share product, catalogue or order information you have asked for.

    Sharing

    We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it with our authorised dealers and distributors only to serve your enquiry, or where required by law.

    Third-party services

    This website links to WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram and online marketplaces. When you use those services, their own privacy policies apply.

    Your choices

    You can ask us to update or delete the information you have shared by writing to info@nationalplasto.com.

    Terms & Conditions

    By using this website you agree to the following terms.

    Product information

    Product photographs, colours, dimensions and specifications are shown for general reference. Actual products may vary slightly in shade and finish, and designs or specifications may be updated without notice. Please confirm details with our sales team or dealer before ordering.

    Prices and orders

    This website does not sell products directly. Prices, availability and delivery are confirmed by NPPL or its authorised dealers at the time of order. Purchases made on third-party marketplaces are governed by that marketplace’s terms.

    Intellectual property

    The NATIONAL, NEXT, CAPTAIN and SAPPHIRE names and logos, product designs, photographs and content on this website belong to National Plasto Products Pvt. Ltd. and may not be copied or used without written permission.

    Governing law

    These terms are governed by the laws of India, and courts at Kolkata, West Bengal shall have jurisdiction.

    Quality Policy

    At National Plasto we are committed to manufacturing plastic moulded furniture that is safe, durable and good value for every customer, at every price point.

    • Consistent quality — following documented processes and inspection at every stage, in line with ISO 9001:2015.
    • Safe workplaces — protecting the health and safety of our people, in line with ISO 45001:2018.
    • Responsible manufacturing — reducing our environmental impact and using energy efficiently, in line with ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 50001:2018.
    • Product safety — meeting CE and BIFMA requirements for the furniture we make.
    • Continual improvement — listening to customers, dealers and employees, and improving our products and processes every year.

    All four of our manufacturing plants — Howrah, Guwahati, Roorkee and Hajipur — are independently certified. View our certificates →
